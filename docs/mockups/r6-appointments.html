How it works

  1. You share one link (Instagram bio, WhatsApp). Clients pick which dietitian's calendar, the visit type, a free time and how to pay.
  2. Only free times inside your working hours show. Booked or blocked times are crossed out.
  3. Pay online (card) or pay at the clinic — you choose per calendar which options to offer. Unpaid visits show “Unpaid” and you can mark them paid by hand (cash / card / transfer).
  4. The new appointment appears in that calendar; the client gets the interview link straight away.
  5. The day before, the appointment shows “Send reminder”: one tap opens WhatsApp with the message ready.

Online payment needs a payment account (for example Stripe, Tap or HyperPay). We'll pick one when we build it; until then “pay at the clinic” works on its own.

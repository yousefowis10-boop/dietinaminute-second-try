How the client app works

  • When you give a client a plan, they get their own private link (sent by WhatsApp). No app store, no password — the link is the key, and you can switch it off any time.
  • They see today's meals, the weekly plan (with swaps you allowed), the shopping list, their workout and their next appointment.
  • They tap a meal when they eat it (tap again for “half”), and tap a glass for each 250 ml of water.
  • “Check in now” opens the check-in you already have (weight, InBody photo).

How the numbers are counted

  • Adherence = meals ticked ÷ meals in the plan for the last 7 days (a “half” counts as half). Shown on the Today screen and on each client's page.
  • Stopped logging = no ticks for 3 days in a row (you can change the number).
  • Follow-up due = no visit for 14 days and nothing booked, or a check-in date that has arrived.
  • Packages and unpaid balances come from the appointments and payments.

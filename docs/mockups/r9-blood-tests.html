How it works
  • Upload a photo or PDF from any lab (also from the client's phone page). The AI reads the test names, results, units and the lab's own normal ranges; you check and save. You can also type tests by hand.
  • Record: every test is kept with the original file, so you see all past results side by side with a trend line.
  • Advice covers the common nutrition-related tests: iron/ferritin, hemoglobin, vitamin D, B12, folate, calcium, magnesium, zinc, glucose/HbA1c, cholesterol and triglycerides, uric acid. Each low/high result gets foods from your food list (marked if already in the plan), simple tips, and one-click “add to plan”.
  • Safety: no doses or medicines; results far outside the range show “refer to doctor”. The client only sees advice after you press “Share with client”.
  • Arabic and English, like the rest of the app. The AI reading is part of Pro; typing results by hand works for everyone.
